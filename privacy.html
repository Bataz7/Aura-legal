Introduction

Welcome to Aura. Aura is a Discord application created to provide moderation, automation, logging, server management, utility tools, and other community features. This Privacy Policy explains how Aura collects, stores, uses, and protects information when users interact with the bot or any related services. Our goal is to collect only the information necessary to provide the requested functionality while respecting user privacy. By using Aura, inviting it to a Discord server, or interacting with any of its commands or services, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to this Privacy Policy.
