Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

Aura may collect limited information required to operate the bot, including Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, command usage, and configuration settings. We do not collect passwords, payment information, or private Discord messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely to provide bot features, improve performance, maintain security, and deliver requested services.

3. Data Storage

Configuration data may be securely stored while the bot is active in your server. Information is protected using reasonable security measures.

4. Third-Party Services

Aura may use trusted third-party services such as Discord APIs, hosting providers, analytics, and databases to operate the service.

5. User Rights

Server owners may request removal of stored configuration data by contacting the Aura development team.

6. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Aura Team through our official support server.
