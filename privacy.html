Introduction

Welcome to Aura. Aura is a Discord bot designed to provide moderation, automation, logging, and server management features. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Aura collects, how it is used, how it is protected, and your rights regarding that information. By inviting or using Aura, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Aura may collect only the information required to provide its services. This may include: • Discord User IDs • Discord Server IDs • Channel IDs • Role IDs • Configuration settings • Moderation logs • Command usage statistics Aura never collects Discord passwords, private messages, payment information, or any information that is not required to provide its services.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely to provide bot features, improve performance, maintain security, and deliver requested services.

How We Use Your Information

Aura uses collected information exclusively to operate and improve the service. Collected information may be used for: • Providing moderation features • Storing server configuration • Processing commands • Preventing abuse and spam • Improving performance and stability • Generating moderation logs Aura never sells personal information to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Data Storage & Security

Aura stores only the information required for its operation. Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures are used to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Although every effort is made to protect your information, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Services

Aura relies on trusted third-party providers to deliver its services. These providers may include: • Discord API • Hosting providers • Database providers • Analytics services These services process information only as necessary to provide Aura's functionality.

Data Retention

Configuration and operational data may be retained while Aura remains installed in your Discord server. When practical and requested, stored configuration may be removed after the bot is removed or upon request by the server owner.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of certain stored information. Requests may be submitted through Aura's official support server.

Children's Privacy

Aura is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the minimum age required by applicable laws or Discord's Terms of Service.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Material changes will become effective immediately after publication unless otherwise stated. Continued use of Aura after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the Aura Team through the official Discord support server. Thank you for trusting Aura.
